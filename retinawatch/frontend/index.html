TRANSPARENCY / 05

Show the work.
Know the limits.

RetinaWatch prioritises visible assumptions: a held-out test set, grade-level metrics, image-quality hints, class-score margin checks and Grad-CAM. These safeguards make the prototype easier to inspect, not clinically proven.

01

Small, imbalanced dataset

3,662 labelled images across five classes. The model’s internal test performance does not tell us how it performs on new hospitals, cameras or populations.

02

Class scores are not calibrated

The highest softmax score is the winning class, not a trustworthy clinical probability. Similar top scores trigger an uncertainty warning.

03

Visual attention is not diagnosis

Grad-CAM shows image regions that affect the model score. It does not prove that a lesion exists in a highlighted region.

04

Professional review matters

Do not use this application to make health decisions. For screening or symptoms, consult a qualified eye-care professional.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE

Academic research prototype — not a medical device.

RetinaWatch is not clinically validated. It can make incorrect predictions, especially for underrepresented grades. Do not use it to diagnose, rule out disease, select treatment or delay medical care. Any health concern should be discussed with an eye-care professional.
